# Competitive Assessment

The current evidence-backed assessment is [AUDIT_2026-09-03.md](AUDIT_2026-09-03.md).

Earlier numeric peer scores were expert estimates rather than controlled benchmark results. They are intentionally retired: repository implementation coverage, local deterministic verification, environment readiness and external empirical proof are separate evidence classes and must not be collapsed into one headline score.

Nakeuri may claim its inspected governance, evidence and lifecycle mechanisms exist and pass the recorded local checks. It may not claim overall superiority over OpenHands, Antigravity, the official Flutter AI stack, VGV Wingspan or OpenAI Symphony until the fair hidden-test comparison contract has been executed on source-identical tasks and the required live runtime/device/release evidence exists.
